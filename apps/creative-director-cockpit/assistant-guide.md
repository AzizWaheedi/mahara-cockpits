# Creative director cockpit: how it works
Written from the code on main, 2026-11-04.

## Who uses it
The creative director and the creative team use it to plan scripts, angles, the social calendar and funnels. The CEO also opens it to see the same work.

## Today (address /dashboard)

**What it shows:**
The day in order on one page, with a short count of Brand DNA gaps, open scripts and late videos under the title. The page is split into four parts: the morning, scripting, the videos and the client touchpoints. It opens with anything late and blocking a client, then your own open tasks, the WhatsApp desk and the fixed checklist. Lower down it shows the scripting calendar, offers waiting to be locked, Brand DNA rows, clients in onboarding, script requests, the video pipeline, who owes what, and every client owed a message.

**Buttons and actions:**
- "Send for review": opens the page that makes one review link for a client.
- "Meetings": opens the meetings and messages page.
- "End of day": opens your end-of-day report page.
- "Morning", "Scripting", "Videos", "Touchpoints": jump to that part of Today.
- A checklist row: saves the tick against your account, so the same row stays ticked when you come back.
- "Write it": opens the suggested message for that client, with the template, the language switch and a text box.
- "English" and "العربية": swap the suggested message between English and Arabic.
- "Copy the message": copies the wording so you can send it in WhatsApp.
- "Log touchpoint": records that you messaged that client today and moves the row to done.
- "Lock the offer": opens the client's page at the offer step.
- "Show fewer": folds the Brand DNA list back to the rows still missing a document.
- "Hide the library": closes the full template library.
- A late item, a script request or a video row: opens its ClickUp task in a new tab.

**How to:**
- Start the day: 1. Open Today. 2. Read the section called "Late and blocking a client" and clear the worst item first. 3. Tick each row under "Before you produce anything". 4. Check "Your tasks" for anything due today.
- Log a client message: 1. Find the client under "Owed a message today". 2. Press "Write it". 3. Pick a template or switch language. 4. Read the warning above the text if one shows. 5. Edit the wording. 6. Press "Copy the message" and send it in WhatsApp. 7. Come back and press "Log touchpoint".
- Plan a script for a client: 1. Go to the "Scripting" part. 2. In "Scripting calendar", pick the client and the day. 3. Write a title or leave it blank. 4. Write the angle in the brief box. 5. Press "Put it on the board".
- Lock an offer after a Brand Blueprint call: 1. Find the client under "Offers to lock". 2. Press "Lock the offer". 3. On the client's page, open the Brand Blueprint card. 4. Fill the offer lines and press "Lock the offer" again.

## Clients (address /clients)

**What it shows:**
One row per live client, with the current status and any work waiting on you. Each row can show open scripts, open videos and a missing document warning. The page has two views. "All clients" is the searchable list you open a client from. "Worst first" shows every client with the most open and oldest work first, and each row folds open to show onboarding, the week's numbers, touchpoints, live ads, the video pipeline and open scripts.

**Buttons and actions:**
- "Practice a Blueprint call": opens the made-up practice client, where nothing is saved or sent.
- "Find a client": filters the list as you type.
- "All clients": shows the plain list.
- "Worst first": shows the folded client cards, worst first.
- A client row: opens that client's page.
- "Open their page": opens the client's page from a folded row.
- "ClickUp record": opens the client's ClickUp card in a new tab.
- "Brand DNA", "Offer sheet", "Market research", "Drive folder": open that document or folder. A document that is not on the card says it is not on file.
- "Run the Blueprint call": opens the Brand Blueprint call page for that client.
- "Script from here", "Their funnel", "Work in flight", "Everything we made", "Talk to them": switch the tab under the client header.
- "To Ideation": saves that ad to the Ideation board under saved ideas.
- "Comment": opens a comment box on a task row.
- "Mark done": moves the ClickUp task to its own done status.
- "Post": posts the comment to ClickUp.
- "Cancel": closes the comment box without posting.
- "New video request": opens the small video request form for that client.
- "Send to the editors": queues a tagged video task for the editors.
- "Open their WhatsApp": opens a WhatsApp chat with the client.
- "Log touchpoint": records a touchpoint for that client.
- "Copy message": copies a suggested message.
- "Open the offer sheet": opens the client's offer document.
- "Lock the offer": sends the locked offer for that client.

**How to:**
- Write a script for a client: 1. Open Clients. 2. Open the client. 3. Read the documents and the "Do's and don'ts" card. 4. Stay on "Script from here". 5. Read the live ads, the history and the proven ads. 6. Write the script in your own tool, then send it to an editor with "New video request".
- Request a video: 1. Open the client. 2. Open "Work in flight". 3. Press "New video request". 4. Pick new video or edit video, and a due date. 5. Check the footage folder link. 6. Write the brief. 7. Press "Send to the editors".
- Comment on a task: 1. Open "Work in flight". 2. Press "Comment" on the row. 3. Type the update. 4. Press "Post".
- Mark work done: 1. Open "Work in flight". 2. Find the row. 3. Press "Mark done". The row says the change is queued and reaches ClickUp within a minute.
- Check what a client already made: 1. Open the client. 2. Open "Everything we made". 3. Read every script request and video job, newest first.

## Library (address /what-works)

**What it shows:**
The Library holds everything you write from, as five tabs across the top. The first tab is the GCC playbook: what to make more of from the live ad accounts, a worth-copying finding, the ad set table by service line and city, what the winning ads look like, and the winning ads word for word. The other tabs are the scripting database, the scripts the team made, the Ideation board and the swipe file.

**Buttons and actions:**
- "What works", "Scripting database", "Scripts we made", "Ideation", "Swipe file": switch to that tab.
- "Every service line" and "Everywhere": filter the ad set table.
- "All", "Saved by the team", "Found by the weekly check": filter the winning ads by where they came from.
- "Saved by anyone": limits the winning ads to one person's saved picks.
- "Read it" and "Hide": open and close one ad's headline, copy, transcript and targeting.
- "Copy this ad": copies the ad's text.
- "Save to Ideation": puts the ad on the Ideation board under saved ideas.

**How to:**
- Find a proven angle for a service line: 1. Open the Library. 2. Stay on "What works". 3. Pick the service line and the city. 4. Read the cheapest rows and the verdict. 5. Press "Read it" on an ad to read its copy.
- Save a winning ad for the team: 1. Find the ad. 2. Press "Read it". 3. Press "Save to Ideation".

## Social (address /social)

**What it shows:**
One client's month as a calendar, with Saturday first. Each cell is a day, and a planned post shows its picture and a coloured state dot. The row above the calendar counts how many posts are in each state and how many are still to plan. Warnings appear above the calendar when a client is not linked to Instagram and Facebook yet, or when a check needs attention. Picking a day on a phone lists that day's posts under the calendar.

**Buttons and actions:**
- The client picker: switches the month to that client. Picking a client who is not on the package adds them to it.
- Previous month and next month arrows: change the month.
- "Today": returns the calendar to the current month.
- "Fill the month": writes drafts across the empty days up to the client's monthly target. They appear as they finish.
- "Client setup": opens the setup sheet for that client.
- "Send to the client": opens the sheet that makes one approval link for the month.
- A day cell: opens that day's first post, or adds a post when the day is empty and still to come.
- "New post": the sheet title for a post you are adding.
- "Make it with AI" and "Use my own": choose whether the pictures are generated or uploaded.
- "Create the post": adds the post to the day and starts writing its captions.
- "Draw the pictures" and "Draw them all again": generate the post's pictures again.
- "Make it move": turns a still picture into a short video.
- "Make a cover": makes a cover from a video.
- "Add words": writes words onto the picture.
- "Remove the post": removes the post from the month after you confirm.
- "Plan", "Look" and "Publishing": group the settings in "Client setup".
- "Add one": adds another post pillar.
- "Photo library": holds the client's own photos. The Add tile uploads more.
- "Posts go to": turns Instagram or Facebook on or off for the client.
- "Post automatically on their day": switches automatic posting on after the readiness steps are done.
- "Goes out without the client's sign-off": lets ready posts publish without client approval.
- "Save": saves the setup.
- "Take off the package": removes the client from the social package after you confirm.
- "Make the link for" and "Copy the link": create and copy the client approval link.
- "Done": closes the approval sheet.

**How to:**
- Plan a client's month: 1. Open Social. 2. Pick the client. 3. Press "Fill the month" or click a day and press "Create the post". 4. Open each post and check its picture and caption. 5. Press "Send to the client" when the month is ready.
- Write one post: 1. Click a day. 2. Choose "Make it with AI" or "Use my own". 3. Describe the post. 4. Pick the format, the shape and the pillar. 5. Press "Create the post".
- Move a post: 1. Drag the post to another day, or open it. 2. Use the date field beside the title. 3. Pick the new day.
- Send the month for approval: 1. Press "Send to the client". 2. Tick the finished posts. 3. Add an optional line for the client. 4. Press "Make the link for" the chosen posts. 5. Press "Copy the link" and send it in WhatsApp.
- Turn on automatic posting: 1. Open "Client setup". 2. Link the Facebook Page and Instagram account. 3. Set the caption dialect and the pillars. 4. Press "Save". 5. Turn on "Post automatically on their day" and confirm.

## Funnels (address /funnels)

**What it shows:**
Where leads come in for every live ad, read from Meta. The top card compares cost per lead against the number of filtering questions on instant forms. The next list holds every destination with its account, spend, leads and cost per lead. The question bank below lists every filtering question already live somewhere in the account, with its answer options and numbers.

**Buttons and actions:**
- A destination row: folds open to show the form headline, the exact filtering questions, the contact questions, the follow-up page and the ads pointing there.
- A button at the end of the list: shows the next 50 destinations.
- A button in the question bank: shows the next 50 questions.
- "Open the page the ads point at": opens the landing page in a new tab.
- "Where it sends them after submitting": opens the page the form sends people to.

**How to:**
- Check whether a form filters its leads: 1. Open Funnels. 2. Find the destination. 3. Open the row. 4. Read the filtering questions. 5. If the list is empty, the page says every click becomes a lead.
- Copy a question into a new form: 1. Scroll to "The question bank". 2. Find the question. 3. Read its answer options and cost per lead. 4. Use the same wording in the new form.

## Links (address /links)

**What it shows:**
Every link you open in a day, grouped into cards: how the cockpit works, the Brand Blueprint call, the two places you file work, what you write from, talking to clients, and the boards. Each row says what the link is for.

**Buttons and actions:**
- A link name: opens the link in a new tab.
- "Copy": copies the link so you can paste it into a message.
- "Watch the demo": plays the cockpit demo in place.
- A link to a cockpit page: opens that page instead of an outside site.

**How to:**
- Find the current link: 1. Open Links. 2. Find the card. 3. Press the link name to open it, or "Copy" to paste it.
- Watch a how-to video: 1. Open Links. 2. Find the video row. 3. Press play.

## Pages you reach from other pages

- End of day, address /eod. You reach it from the "End of day" button on Today. It counts the day from the boards and lets you write tomorrow's plan and a private draft report. Buttons: "Add", "Remove", "Save a draft" and "Update the draft".
- Send for review, address /review. You reach it from the "Send for review" button on Today. It makes one link the client can open to approve or comment on videos and images. Buttons: "Add another", "Make the link", "Copy" and "See what they see".
- Meetings and messages, address /meetings. You reach it from the "Meetings" button on Today. It shows today's meetings, the next call per client, WhatsApp threads waiting on you, quiet clients, the next seven days and all threads. Buttons: "Join", "Send on WhatsApp", "Connect your Google Calendar", "Disconnect" and "Connect".
- Scripting database, address /scripting. You reach it from the Library tab row. It lists proven ads you can script from, searchable by hook, copy and transcript. Buttons: "Only ads still live" and the winner filters.
- Scripts we made, address /scripts. You reach it from the Library tab row. It lists every completed script task with its ClickUp text. Buttons: "Copy the script", "Back to the ClickUp text", "Open in ClickUp", "Send to the editors", "Open the form", "Show the form here" and "Hide the form".
- Ideation, address /ideation. You reach it from the Library tab row. It holds saved ideas, proposals from the scan, trends, posts still being fetched, failed posts and dismissed posts. Buttons: "Refresh", "Keys", "Fetch and save", "Scrape", "Add", "Keep it", "Try again", "Restore", "Read it", "Open the post" and "Save note".
- Swipe file, address /swipe. You reach it from the Library tab row. It shows the ads the team saved in Foreplay, longest running first. Buttons: "Send to ideation", "Open in Foreplay", "Landing page" and "On air 60+ days".
- A client's page, address /clients/:name. You reach it by opening a client from Clients, or from a client link on Today or in the search box.
- The Brand Blueprint call, address /blueprint/:taskId. You reach it from "Run the Blueprint call" on a client's page or from an offer waiting on Today. It opens the prep with a rail for the three parts: "Starter ads", "Brand and money" and "The offer". Buttons: "Open the notes window", "Reload" and the part buttons "Send to media", "Close the call" and "Lock the offer". While presenting, use "Present again" and "Back to prep".
- The practice Blueprint call, address /blueprint/practice. You reach it from "Practice a Blueprint call" on Clients. It runs the same call on a made-up client, and nothing is saved or sent.
- The Brand Blueprint notes window, address /blueprint/:taskId/notes. You reach it from "Open the notes window" on the call page. It shows the notes on their own, with no menu.
- The practice notes window, address /blueprint/practice/notes. It is the notes window for the practice call.
- Settings, address /settings. You reach it from the account menu at the bottom of the menu. It holds your account, the appearance switch, the Brand Blueprint settings, "Change password" and "Delete account".
- Old addresses /work, /calendar, /touchpoints and /messages land on the matching part of Today. The old address /profiles lands on the "Worst first" view of Clients. Old bookmarks keep working.

## While a page loads

- When a page or section first loads, placeholder blocks show its layout. The blocks are not data and hold no numbers.
- On a refresh in the background, data you already saw stays on screen. This applies to pages whose data hook keeps the last data.
- Save, upload and send actions show progress while they run. Wait for the result before you act on it.

## Everywhere in the cockpit

- "How to use this cockpit": the menu row near the bottom. It opens the cockpit's written guide in ClickUp in a new tab.
- "Team meetings": opens the team meetings page on the portal.
- "Ask a team": opens the portal page for asking another team for help. It files the request in ClickUp for you.
- "Client performance": opens the portal board that shows every client's performance.
- "Admin": shown to admins. It opens the portal admin view.
- The other cockpit doors: "Media buyer", "Client success", "Editor desk" and "Sales" appear only for people who hold that role or are admins. Each one opens that cockpit.
- The chat button at the bottom right: opens Lens, the creative assistant. It answers questions about creative requests, ads and hooks, and it reads the page you are on. It is hidden on the Brand Blueprint call page.
- Now and then a small bubble above the chat button suggests one question for the page you are on: click it to ask that question, or press the X to hide it. It shows at most once per page a day, and two dismissals in a row hide it for a week.
- "New chat": clears the conversation with Lens and starts again.
- "Copy answer", "Helpful" and "Not helpful": copy an answer or rate it.
- "Search…": the field at the top of the menu, or the same search with Ctrl K. It finds a client, a page or a link.
- "Alerts": the bell in the top bar on a phone and a row on a wide screen. It shows what changed for you in ClickUp, with "Mark all read" at the bottom.
- "Your tasks": the card near the top of Today. It shows your open ClickUp tasks, and "See all" opens the rest in a panel.
- The account menu: at the bottom of the menu. It holds "Settings", "Dark mode" or "Light mode", "Mahara portal" and "Sign out".

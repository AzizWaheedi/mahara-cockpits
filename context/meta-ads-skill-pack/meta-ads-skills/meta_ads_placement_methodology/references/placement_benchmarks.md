# 2026 Placement Benchmarks by Industry

## Overview

These benchmarks represent cross-industry averages and industry-specific data for Meta Ads placements as of Q1 2026. Use them as directional reference points, not absolute targets. Your account's actual performance depends on creative quality, audience, product-market fit, and account maturity.

**Data sources:** Aggregated from industry reports, Meta's own published benchmarks, and practitioner networks. Updated quarterly.

## Seasonal Variations

### CPM Index by Quarter (100 = Q1 baseline)

| Placement | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 |
|-----------|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Facebook Feed | 100 | 110 | 105 | 140 |
| Instagram Feed | 100 | 108 | 103 | 135 |
| Stories (all) | 100 | 105 | 100 | 125 |
| Reels (all) | 100 | 103 | 98 | 120 |
| Audience Network | 100 | 107 | 102 | 130 |

**Key seasonal patterns:**
- Q1 (Jan-Mar): Lowest CPMs. Post-holiday dip. Best time for testing and prospecting.
- Q2 (Apr-Jun): Moderate increase. Spring shopping, graduation season.
- Q3 (Jul-Sep): Relatively flat. Back-to-school spike in August.
- Q4 (Oct-Dec): Highest CPMs. Black Friday week can spike 50-80% above baseline. December slightly lower than November.
- Election years (US): +15-30% CPM spike in October-November for all placements.

## How to Use These Benchmarks

### Do

- Use as directional context when evaluating your own placement data
- Compare your placement-level performance to industry benchmarks to identify outliers
- Use seasonal indices to plan budget allocation across quarters
- Reference when explaining placement performance to stakeholders

### Don't

- Use these as hard targets (your account will differ)
- Compare a single day to these averages (too much variance)
- Use to justify excluding placements (a placement below benchmark may still be profitable)
- Treat as static (benchmarks shift quarterly, review updated data regularly)

### Your Own Benchmarks

After running campaigns for 30+ days with 100+ conversions:
1. Export your placement breakdown data
2. Calculate your own CTR, CPC, CPM, CVR, and CPA by placement
3. Compare to these industry benchmarks to identify overunder_performers
4. Use your own data as the primary benchmark going forward
5. Revisit industry benchmarks quarterly to check for market shifts

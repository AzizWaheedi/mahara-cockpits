## Changes

- Split Admin into separately authenticated queries, pause expensive subscriptions while hidden, and load client names only when editing access.
- Replace growing AI job-history reads with indexed summaries and transactional counters. Keep the compatible overview and a fallback until the additive backfill completes.
- Coalesce repeated healthy writes while preserving immediate failure and recovery updates. Index idle queues, relays and job lookups; skip unchanged calendar status writes.
- Preserve the previous daily/booking grain when no campaigns were fetched. Let winner archiving catch up after a missed daily slot.
- Apply the agreed USD 15 cost-per-lead and USD 60 cost-per-booking gates throughout the remaining consumers.
- Keep retention forced to dry run, with no cleanup cron or deletions.

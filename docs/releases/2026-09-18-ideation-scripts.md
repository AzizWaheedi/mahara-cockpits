# Ideation board, scrapes and ad libraries, Scripts we made, show rate

Aziz asked for these on 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-18; Claude built and shipped them the same days. Both the creative director and media buyer cockpits carry the Ideation changes; the scripts page and the Save to Ideation buttons are creative only.

- Ideation board (`/ideation` in both cockpits): a Trends tab (the same format on three accounts or more inside two weeks, flagged by the radar after every scan), a three-frame storyboard instead of one still, keyboard shortcuts (`?` lists them), and platform chips for YouTube, Facebook, Meta ads and Google ads.
- Scrape box on the board: paste any creator or brand page (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat) or name a page, advertiser or keyword for the Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center. The radar on the VPS runs it within two minutes (`ideation_requests`), proposes the page's best videos, watches the account, pulls the brand's current Meta ads, and ranks ads by how long they have run: proposed from 7 days, "study" from 21, "reverse engineer" from 60. Recent scrapes show their status and result under the box.
- Watchlist panel on the board: accounts, hashtags and Instagram keyword searches, added and removed there.
- Save to Ideation next to every scripting database row and every live or past ad on a client's page (creative cockpit). Our own ads land as saved ideas with the client, the script when the archive has it, and spend, leads and cost per lead.
- Scripts we made (`/scripts`, creative cockpit): every script task marked complete on the ClickUp creative board with its text, editable in place, one button that sends it to the editors through the existing video request outbox with the client's footage folder filled in, and the ClickUp video request form embedded. No AI drafting: Aziz decided the creative director keeps writing with his own LLM.
- Show rate on the creative client page: shows divided by appointments that came due (date passed, Show column filled), not by every booking. The media buyer's stat sheet reader reports `due` next to `booked`.
- Radar (`hermes/ideation-radar`): ElevenLabs Scribe first for speech (it beat Whisper and Gemini on ten Gulf clips and stays silent on silent clips), Gemini watches the video with that transcript; trends through Gemini or OpenAI descriptors and embeddings; Instagram keyword search targets that grow the watchlist by themselves; digest to Aziz and Sabry; `doctor` lines for ElevenLabs, embeddings and ScrapeCreators credits.

Backend and schema: Supabase `ideation_posts` gained trend, ad, client and numbers columns; `ideation_requests` is new; `creativeTasks.script` (creative cockpit) carries the ClickUp description of script tasks; the creative stats validator gained `due`. New Convex functions: `ideation.watchlistList|watchlistAdd|watchlistRemove|requestScrape|requestsList` (both cockpits), `ideation.saveFromWinner|saveFromClientAd` and `scripts.list` (creative). No KPI, campaign execution or permission changes.

Validation: 66 radar tests; biome and `tsc` clean in both apps; production smoke checks green after every ship; live checks on the VPS for the trend step, a Scribe-first capture with a storyboard, a page scrape that found the page's Meta ads by its Instagram handle, and a keyword ad pull. Found on the way: the Vercel CLI prints JSON off a terminal and one deploy silently did nothing until `scripts/ship.sh` was made to verify the confirmation; Gemini's daily quota ran out under test load and every step fell back as designed.

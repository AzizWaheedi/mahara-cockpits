## Composition

- Open on client context and this offer's title. Dated captures are optional evidence except where server readiness requires them; absent data is never invented.
- Walk the offer-specific visual with one deliverable revealed at a time. A final stop names what is needed from the client.
- Show the real process as a sequence with the approved words.
- Show the investment from that offer's parts and cadence. No annualisation for day or 90-day work; no invented returns.
- Close with the approved commitment and question. Keep the process/video reveal and presenter notes separate from the proposal.

The shared controls and saved-data model stay stable. Structural variety lives in the presentation's subject matter, not alternate fonts or colors. The branded mark is genuine; the parent builder owns public proposal branding and verified media.

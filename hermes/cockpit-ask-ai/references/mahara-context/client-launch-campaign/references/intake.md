# Intake

Ask all of it before touching the API. Write the answers into the brief JSON as
you go. An unanswered field is better than a guessed one, because a blank tells
the next person to go ask, and a guess looks like fact.

## Client and account

1. Client name, exactly as it should appear in campaign names.
2. Ad account ID. **Get this from the client database sheet, not from the ad
   account list.** Names repeat across accounts and picking by eye is how you
   launch on the wrong client's budget.
3. Facebook Page ID that the ads run from. Confirm it is the client's own page.
4. Which country and currency the account runs in.

## Service and offer

5. What is the one service this campaign sells? One only. A launch campaign
   that sells four services teaches you nothing about any of them.
6. What does the prospect actually get when they book? The deliverables, not the
   service name. This is what the copy is built from.
7. Is the consultation free? If not, do not write مجاناً anywhere.
8. Any claims that are off limits. For licensed professions there are usually
   several: approval outcomes, permit timelines, guarantees, penalty amounts.

## Consultation type, which sets the radius

9. Ask: in-office, in-home, online, or a combination?

| Answer | Radius |
|---|---|
| In-office only | 25km around the office |
| In-home only | 40km around the service area |
| Both office and home | 40km |
| Online | **ask, never assume** |

Online does not mean nationwide. Plenty of clients take online consultations and
still only want their own city, because their team cannot service further out
even when the first call is remote. Ask which regions they actually want and
list them explicitly.

10. What is the exact address or city centre the radius is measured from?
11. Any cities to exclude?

## Budget

12. Daily budget in the account currency.
    - $25 to $50: standard. One CBO campaign, one ad set.
    - Above $50: same structure, bigger number. Do not add ad sets.
    - Below $25: say plainly that lead-gen optimisation struggles to exit the
      learning phase at that level, and ask whether they can start at $25.

## Lead form

13. Ask: reuse an existing form, or build a new one?

Never decide this silently. An old form can point at a disconnected CRM, or ask
questions that no longer match the offer, and the leads land nowhere.

- **Reusing:** get the form ID, then confirm it is Active and that its questions
  still match this offer.
- **New:** build it with them from `form_template.md`. Do not create one
  unilaterally.

14. Where do leads need to arrive? CRM, sheet, WhatsApp, email. Confirm the
    integration exists before launch, not after.

## Assets

15. Ask for a Google Drive folder link, or a direct upload into the chat.
16. How many usable assets are there? Five ads needs five. If there are three,
    say so and build three rather than padding with weak ones.
17. Is the client's logo in the folder, and may it be placed on the creative?
18. For videos: confirm they are the client's own footage and cleared for ads.

**Read every asset before writing copy.** Look at each image. Transcribe every
video. The copy has to match the specific asset it sits on, and you cannot match
what you have not looked at.

## Language and voice

19. Which language do the ads run in?
20. If Arabic, **which dialect**? A Saudi client reads Hejazi or Najdi, a Kuwaiti
    client reads Kuwaiti. Do not default to Aziz's Kuwaiti voice for a client
    campaign, and do not default to MSA either unless the audience is formal.
21. Any words the client refuses to use, and any competitor language to avoid.

## Numbers and proof

22. Which numbers may appear in an ad? Years in business, projects delivered,
    team size, response time.

Anything not on this list does not go in an ad. Not a rounded version of it, not
an approximation, not a number from the website that nobody confirmed. If the
copy needs a number and you do not have one, write around it.
